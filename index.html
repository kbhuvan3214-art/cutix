Questions patients ask

How do I book a consultation?

Use the form below, call the clinic or message on WhatsApp. The clinic will confirm your time.

What happens in the first consultation?

The doctor listens to your concern, examines your skin or hair, and discusses suitable options.

How should I prepare?

Note your concerns and when they began. Bring details of medicines or products you currently use.

How many sessions will I need?

Treatment requirements vary from person to person and are determined during consultation.

Is every treatment suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability depends on your skin, hair and health, which the doctor assesses first.
